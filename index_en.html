Background & Purpose
AI-based document support tools are increasingly being considered for adoption in quality and certification work environments. However, actual use remains limited due to insufficient trust in AI within high-responsibility, high-stakes contexts.

This study examines how the explainability (XAI) of AI document support tools affects trust and intention to use among quality and certification professionals, and how this relationship varies depending on workplace responsibility level.
